DevOps in the Software Development Lifecycle
Author: Ethan Hakaj (101465282)

DevOps connects development and operations through shared responsibility,
automation, and feedback. During planning, teams define requirements and
acceptance criteria. During implementation, small commits make changes easier
to inspect and recover.

Continuous integration validates proposed changes automatically. Failures give
contributors feedback before merging. Continuous delivery prepares validated
changes for release; continuous deployment also releases them automatically.
A successful CI check alone does not prove a deployed service is online.

After release, monitoring and incident reviews inform the next planning cycle.
A rollback restores a known working version during investigation. This text-only
project checks required files and unresolved conflict markers; it does not
deploy an application or monitor uptime.
